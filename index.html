    Chesterfield County School District — Purchasing Card Policy

    By acknowledging below, I confirm that I have completed the required P-Card training and agree to abide by the Chesterfield County School District Purchasing Card Policy, as approved by Dr. Anderson on August 6, 2024.

    I understand the P-Card is issued in my name and I am the ONLY person authorized to use it. Sharing or loaning my card is strictly prohibited.

    I understand the P-Card is for official District business only. Personal purchases are prohibited and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

    I understand all receipts must be maintained for 4 years and submitted to my Bookkeeper with the monthly Statement of Account.

    I understand failure to comply may result in reduction of spending limits, card suspension, card cancellation, required reimbursement to the District, and/or termination.

    I understand suspected fraud or misuse must be reported to the P-Card Administrator immediately.
